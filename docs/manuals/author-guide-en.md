# IWSO 2027 — Author Guide

This guide covers everything an author needs to submit an abstract, track its status, and respond to a decision.

## 1. Signing in

IWSO 2027 uses passwordless sign-in — there is no account to register in advance and no password to remember.

1. Go to the sign-in page and enter your email address.
2. Click **Send sign-in link**.
3. Check your inbox for an email from IWSO 2027 and click the link inside it.
4. You'll be signed in automatically. The link expires after a while and can only be used once, so request a new one if it doesn't work.

Your account is created automatically the first time you sign in — there's no separate registration step.

> Note: for spam-prevention reasons, you can only request one sign-in email per minute for the same address. If you just requested one, wait a minute before trying again.

## 2. Submitting an abstract

1. Once signed in, go to **Submissions** in the top menu, then **New Submission** (or **Submit an Abstract** from the homepage).
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** (required)
   - **Track** (required) — choose the topic area that best fits your submission
   - **Presentation Type** — Oral or Poster
   - **Keywords** — up to 10, used later for searching/filtering
   - **Authors** — add every author with name, email, and (optional) affiliation; mark the corresponding author
   - **Abstract PDF** — upload your abstract as a PDF file, **10MB maximum**. Title, authors, and affiliation are entered as text above — the PDF itself only needs to contain the abstract content.
3. Choose one of:
   - **Save Draft** — saves your progress without submitting. You can come back and finish later. Drafts are not sent for review.
   - **Submit** — finalizes your submission and sends it for review. A PDF is required before you can submit.

You'll receive a confirmation email the first time your submission is marked as submitted.

## 3. Editing or withdrawing

Go to **Submissions** to see all your submissions and their status.

- While a submission is still **Draft** or **Submitted** (i.e. before a reviewer has been assigned), you can open it and edit any field, including replacing the PDF.
- You can **Withdraw** a submission any time up until a decision has been recorded — including while it's **Under Review**. Once it's **Decided**, it can no longer be withdrawn.
- Withdrawing cannot be undone, and a withdrawn submission can't be resubmitted — create a new one if needed.

## 4. Submission statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Saved but not yet submitted |
| Submitted | Sent for review, not yet assigned/reviewed |
| Under Review | At least one reviewer has been assigned |
| Decided | The Chair has recorded an Accept/Reject decision |
| Withdrawn | You withdrew it |

## 5. Getting your decision

Once the Chair finalizes a decision, you'll receive an email stating whether your submission was **accepted** or **rejected**, along with any comments reviewers left for authors (confidential comments to the Chair are never shared with you). You can also see the decision on the **Submissions** page at any time — no need to wait for the email.

## 6. What you can't see

To keep the review process fair, authors cannot see:
- Who reviewed their submission, or their scores
- Reviewers' confidential comments to the Chair
- The conference program (published separately on the official conference website)
